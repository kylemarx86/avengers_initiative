| Synopsis2           = Zahner takes the bound Senator Wycombe to the FSSI, where Railsback puts him through a procedure with Nurse Goren; Interestingly, they believe they have replicated Sallis's formula in Wycombe, despite the fact that Wycombe is not a supporter. Meanwhile, in the swamp, the Man-Thing begins to regain his mind and remembers that his name is Ted and that he is close to "something". Senator Miller tells journalist Mickey Ditillio that he doesn't know anything about Project: Glamor and advises him not to publish about it; but Ditillio investigates and looks for links between FSSI; the guerrillas and anti-drug operations; banking operations in Colombia and Switzerland, and Latverian Arms Sales; suspecting that Choate is involved and that he is connected to Sallis's investigation. In Maryland, Colonel Choate (aka Steel) activates "the demon" to solve his problems. In the swamp, the Man-Thing interrupts a coven that was going to sacrifice a puppy, but the witch in charge of it (Marea), not really understanding Man-Thing, manages to convince him to "lead" her.
